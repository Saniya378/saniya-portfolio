Background & Story

My Journey

I am a passionate Junior Full-Stack & Mobile Developer with a Bachelor's in Information Technology from Karakoram International University (KIU), Gilgit. My academic journey, combined with hands-on project experience, has prepared me to build real-world software that matters.

My engineering journey bridges the gap between scalable backend systems and seamless client applications. On the web side, I specialise in the Laravel & PHP ecosystem, crafting secure RESTful APIs using Laravel Sanctum, implementing granular role-based access control (RBAC), designing normalised relational schemas with MySQL, and templating dynamic interfaces with Laravel Blade. I embrace modern Laravel 11 & 12 conventions — from service containers to eloquent relationships — writing maintainable, testable code from day one.

On mobile, I build polished, cross-platform Android applications with Flutter & Dart. I architect my apps following Clean Architecture (domain / data / presentation layers), manage reactive state with Riverpod, handle complex HTTP flows through Dio interceptors, and wire declarative routing via GoRouter. The result: apps that are offline-aware, fast to load, and easy to extend.

I am also deeply interested in AI integration. My Final Year Project — Book My Beauty — embeds the Google Gemini API as a smart salon consultation assistant, letting users receive intelligent beauty recommendations in a conversational chat interface. This experience ignited a passion for making software smarter and more intuitive through generative AI.

Beyond software development, I have been a dedicated Home Tutor since 2020, teaching primary and secondary school students across Gilgit. This experience has sharpened my communication, structured problem-solving, and leadership skills — qualities I bring directly into team collaborations and code reviews. It has also cultivated deep patience and the ability to explain complex topics clearly, something invaluable in software engineering.

I am a proud native of Gilgit-Baltistan — one of Pakistan's most breathtaking regions — and I bring that mountain-tenacity and resilience into every challenge I tackle as a developer. I am actively seeking Junior Developer roles and internships where I can contribute meaningfully while continuing to grow.
